Architecture notes

The three things that actually matter

The division

Not a tax engine

The app is the custody, exchange and relationship layer — files, messages, signatures, answers and money. The return is prepared in the firm's software by a person.

The hard part

Signing is the regulated bit

Remote signing of Form 8879 needs knowledge-based identity verification, a three-attempt limit, a fallback to wet signature, and a tamper-proof three-year evidence record. That is the piece to buy, not build.

The obligation

Building it means owning it

A firm that runs its own portal is a financial institution under the FTC Safeguards Rule: a named Qualified Individual, written risk assessment, MFA, encryption, annual penetration testing, and a 30-day breach clock.
